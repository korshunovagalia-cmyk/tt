Our standards

5 steps to perfection.

What to expect when you work with us.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    Send photos of your windows or book a site visit for a fast, accurate quote. For larger or commercial jobs, we assess access, glass type and site conditions in person.

  2. 02

    Film specification

    Based on your priorities — heat, privacy, budget or appearance — we recommend the right film tier and shade, with samples available to view before you commit.

  3. 03

    Installation

    Installed by an experienced team, with commercial and façade work scheduled to minimise disruption to your business or building.

  4. 04

    Final inspection

    Every job is checked on completion for a clean, bubble-free, edge-to-edge finish before we consider it done.

  5. 05

    Warranty & quality control

    All work is backed by a manufacturer warranty (2 years to lifetime, depending on film tier) covering bubbling, discolouration and other film defects from the date of installation.
