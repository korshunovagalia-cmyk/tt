The Science Behind Your Comfort

How solar control films work: they block solar heat, glare and harmful UV rays, keeping your space cooler, brighter and more comfortable.

Glass without solar control film

Solar energy passes through, causing heat, glare and UV exposure.

Glass with solar control film

Film blocks, reflects and absorbs heat, glare and UV rays.

Visible light (what you see)5% – 95% blocked

Reduces glare and controls brightness.

Solar heat (IRR)Up to 99% blocked

Cuts solar heat for a cooler space.

UV rays (harmful)Up to 99% blocked

Blocks harmful UV rays.
