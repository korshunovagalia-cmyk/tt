Our founder

Personal accountability on every project.

Serving the UAE since 2012, Tint and Sun specialises in solar control window films, decorative finishes and customised graphics for vehicles, homes and commercial spaces.

We combine technical assessment, tailored film selection, precise installation and dedicated warranty support to enhance comfort, privacy and appearance.

At the heart of the business is Oksana L., Owner and Solar Control Film Specialist. Progressing from customer care through marketing to managing partner and sole owner, she brings extensive industry experience, technical expertise and personal accountability to every project.

Her leadership has also developed corporate relationships and staff programmes spanning:

  • Emirates Airlines
  • BMW Group Middle East
  • Etihad Airways
  • Australian Consulate-General Dubai
  • American University in Dubai (AUD)
  • Neuron LLC
  • Dubai Aviation Corporation
  • Foilx Custom Workshops
  • ULB Fine

Customer satisfaction and the continued confidence of our partners remain our foremost priorities. This commitment to quality, attentive service and lasting relationships has shaped our reputation across the UAE.

Backed by 14 years of UAE experience, we are committed to dependable service, lasting quality and your continued confidence.
